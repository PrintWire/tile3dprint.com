How Printed Tile Is Made
Step 01

Print

Stone or ceramic paste is extruded into each tile's relief, or powder is binder-jet bound to shape.

Step 02

Cure

Printed tiles cure and gain green strength before finishing or firing.

Step 03

Fire & Seal

Ceramic tiles are fired to vitrification; stone tiles are ground and sealed for wear.
